# Opportunities

Opportunities are potential value-creating situations identified during intelligence gathering. They represent areas where a customer's business pain, strategic priority, or operational gap aligns with your solution capabilities.

## Who this is for

<span class="vp-badge vp-badge--role">End User</span>
<span class="vp-badge vp-badge--role">Admin</span>
<span class="vp-badge vp-badge--role">Executive</span>

## Prerequisites

- A ValuePact account with enrichment data
- Signals or source documents ingested for the account
- A configured value pack with use cases and capabilities

## Overview

Opportunities emerge from the intersection of account intelligence and your product ontology. The platform uses Layer 4 agentic workflows to generate, score, and rank opportunities so you focus on the highest-potential value conversations.

## Identification

Opportunities are identified from three sources:

1. **Signal extraction** — AI analyzes ingested documents, earnings calls, and news to detect business pains and strategic initiatives.
2. **Hypothesis generation** — The platform maps detected signals to your capabilities and proposes value hypotheses.
3. **Whitespace analysis** — A Layer 4 workflow compares the account's stated needs to your solution coverage and flags gaps.

Each opportunity includes:

- **Title** — a concise value statement
- **Description** — the business context and proposed solution
- **Linked signals** — the intelligence that supports the opportunity
- **Linked evidence** — documents or excerpts that ground the claim
- **Confidence score** — 0.0 to 1.0, derived from signal strength and source quality
- **Value driver** — the category of value (revenue, cost, risk, capital)

## Qualification

After identification, opportunities move through qualification:

| Stage | Criteria | Exit action |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Draft | Auto-generated by AI | Review and validate signals |
| Qualified | Signals are relevant and timely | Link to a value hypothesis |
| Prioritized | High confidence and high potential value | Add to the value model |
| Rejected | Low relevance or outdated | Archive with reason |

To qualify an opportunity:

1. Open the account's **Intelligence** workspace.
2. Click the **Value Hypotheses** tab.
3. Review the opportunity's linked signals and evidence.
4. Click **Validate** if the signals support the claim, or **Reject** with a note.
5. Validated opportunities are promoted to the value model.

## Scoring

The platform scores each opportunity on two axes:

- **Confidence** — strength of evidence and signal quality
- **Value potential** — estimated annual benefit based on formula defaults

The score matrix places opportunities into quadrants:

| | High value potential | Low value potential |
|---|---------------------|---------------------|
| **High confidence** | Prioritize first | Quick wins |
| **Low confidence** | Investigate further | Deprioritize |

## Prioritization

Prioritized opportunities feed the driver tree in the value model. You can reorder them by:

- Estimated impact
- Confidence score
- Customer readiness
- Strategic alignment

The top opportunities become the foundation for [ROI calculations](roi-calculations.md) and [business cases](business-cases.md).

### Opportunity lifecycle

```
IDENTIFIED → QUALIFIED → PRIORITIZED → CONVERTED → ARCHIVED
    ↓            ↓             ↓
  REJECTED    STALLED      DEPRIORITIZED
```

- **Identified** — Auto-generated by signal extraction or whitespace analysis
- **Qualified** — Reviewed and confirmed relevant by a user
- **Prioritized** — Added to the value model and assigned a rank
- **Converted** — Linked to a value driver and included in a business case
- **Archived** — No longer relevant or superseded by a newer opportunity
- **Rejected** — Explicitly dismissed with a reason
- **Stalled** — Awaiting customer input or external event
- **Deprioritized** — Valid but lower priority than competing opportunities

Opportunity status is displayed in the Intelligence workspace and filtered in pipeline reports.

## Limits and guardrails

<span class="vp-badge vp-badge--limit">Limit</span> A maximum of 50 active opportunities per account. Archive old ones to make room.

<span class="vp-badge vp-badge--limit">Limit</span> Hypothesis generation is limited to 20 hypotheses per request.

<span class="vp-badge vp-badge--limit">Limit</span> Opportunities with confidence below 0.5 are hidden from the default view but remain searchable.

## Troubleshooting

??? question "Issue: No opportunities generated for an account"
    **Cause:** The account lacks ingested signals, or the value pack does not map to the account's industry.
    **Resolution:** Run **Enrichment** in the Intelligence workspace, or verify the account industry and value pack in **Account Settings**.

??? question "Issue: Opportunity confidence score seems too high"
    **Cause:** A single strong signal may inflate confidence if corroboration is missing.
    **Resolution:** Inspect the opportunity's evidence sources. If only one source exists, add independent evidence or manually lower the confidence in **Edit Opportunity**.

??? question "Issue: Validated opportunity does not appear in the value model"
    **Cause:** The opportunity was validated but not explicitly linked to a value driver.
    **Resolution:** Open the opportunity, click **Link to Driver**, select the relevant driver, and save.

??? question "Issue: Opportunity pipeline shows stale opportunities from last quarter"
    **Cause:** Opportunities are not auto-archived when signals age out.
    **Resolution:** Set an auto-archive policy in **Administration > Configuration > Opportunities**, or manually archive outdated opportunities.

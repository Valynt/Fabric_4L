# Executive Adoption

## Overview

Value realization starts at the top. Executive sponsorship drives platform usage, data quality, and cross-functional alignment. This page outlines how to secure and sustain leadership engagement.

## Who this is for

- <span class="vp-badge vp-badge--role">Executive</span>
- <span class="vp-badge vp-badge--role">Admin</span>
- <span class="vp-badge vp-badge--role">End User</span>

## Prerequisites

- Defined business case for ValuePact within your organization.
- Named executive sponsor with allocated time.
- Baseline metrics to compare against after rollout.

## Step-by-step instructions

### 1. Secure a named executive sponsor

1. Identify a C-level or VP-level leader who benefits directly from value reporting.
2. Document the sponsor’s responsibilities: attending kickoff, reviewing monthly dashboards, and removing blockers.
3. Add the sponsor as an owner on the top-level portfolio.

### 2. Align metrics to strategic priorities

1. Map ValuePact initiatives to the organization's top-three strategic goals.
2. Limit the initial dashboard to five key metrics.
3. Present the metrics in the sponsor’s preferred format (weekly email, Slack digest, or board deck).

### 3. Run a structured change management program

1. Create a communication plan with pre-launch, launch, and post-launch phases.
2. Train champions in each department. Champions answer tier-1 questions and escalate feedback.
3. Schedule a 30-day checkpoint to review adoption metrics and address resistance.

### 4. Celebrate early wins

1. Identify one initiative that will show value within the first 60 days.
2. Publish a short narrative: the problem, the intervention, and the outcome.
3. Share the win in all-hands or executive staff meetings.

### 5. Embed ValuePact into operating rhythm

1. Add portfolio review to the existing executive meeting cadence.
2. Assign a scribe to update initiative status before each meeting.
3. Use the **Executive Dashboard** as the single source of truth for value conversations.

## Troubleshooting

??? question "Issue: Executive sponsor disengages after launch"
    **Cause:** The sponsor does not see personal relevance, or the time commitment was underestimated.
    **Resolution:**
    1. Reschedule check-ins to 15 minutes.
    2. Reframe metrics around the sponsor’s specific OKRs.
    3. Delegate operational tasks to a program manager.

??? question "Issue: Department heads resist entering data"
    **Cause:** They perceive the platform as extra work with no direct benefit.
    **Resolution:**
    1. Auto-populate fields through integrations.
    2. Show how their data feeds upward into resource allocation decisions.
    3. Reduce required fields to the minimum viable set.

# Production Readiness Risk Register

| ID | Area | Severity | Owner | Status | Risk | Validation command | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PRR-001 | Security | [BLOCKER:P0] | Security Engineering | OPEN | A failing security regression, unresolved secret exposure, or weakened auth/RBAC control can expose tenant or customer data. | `pnpm test:security` | Security tests pass and evidence artifact is attached to the scorecard. |
| PRR-002 | Tenancy | [BLOCKER:P0] | Platform Security | OPEN | Missing or stale tenant isolation evidence can allow cross-tenant read or write regressions to reach production. | `pnpm test:isolation` | Tenant isolation gate passes and route tenant propagation evidence is retained. |
| PRR-003 | Reliability | [BLOCKER:P0] | Platform Engineering | OPEN | Production-like golden path or core service failures can make launch unusable even when isolated checks pass. | `python scripts/ci/generate_launch_evidence_bundle.py --artifacts-only --up-to-stage staging` | Launch evidence bundle records passing production-like evidence for core GA blockers. |
| PRR-004 | DR | [BLOCKER:P0] | SRE | OPEN | Backup existence without restore proof creates unrecoverable launch risk. | `pnpm ops:backup:verify` | Restore dry-run and rollback verification artifacts are retained. |
| PRR-005 | Release Safety | [BLOCKER:P0] | Release Engineering | OPEN | Failed release policy, branch protection, immutable image, SBOM, or rollback evidence can make launch unsafe. | `pnpm release:dry-run` | Release dry-run, rollback verification, and deploy evidence artifacts are retained. |
| PRR-006 | Observability | [BLOCKER:P1] | SRE | OPEN | Missing dashboards, alert metadata, or receiver evidence reduces incident detection and response confidence. | `pnpm test:observability` | Observability tests pass and receiver/dashboard evidence links are added to the scorecard. |
| PRR-007 | Billing | [BLOCKER:P1] | Product Engineering | OPEN | Paid launch without metering, entitlement, idempotency, and reconciliation evidence risks revenue and customer trust. | `python scripts/ci/generate_billing_evidence.py --help` | Billing regression evidence is present or paid launch is explicitly out of scope. |
| PRR-008 | Compliance Evidence | [BLOCKER:P1] | Compliance Owner | OPEN | Control evidence without owner review is not auditable for SOC2/ISO-style readiness. | `python scripts/ci/validate_production_readiness_plan.py` | Compliance owner signs off and retained evidence location is recorded. |
| PRR-009 | Data Lifecycle | [BLOCKER:P1] | Data Platform | OPEN | Stale migration, backup, retention, or deletion evidence can create data integrity and governance gaps. | `pnpm db:migrate:check` | Migration and data lifecycle evidence is current and linked from the scorecard. |

# 02 — Users and Jobs

Source: Master Product Intent §2, §2.1, §1.4 (S1).

Primary author: a **value engineer or solutions consultant**. Other personas collaborate on the same durable case with role-appropriate views and actions.

## Personas

| Persona | Primary job | Critical product need |
|---|---|---|
| Value Engineer or Solutions Consultant | Build and defend a customer-specific value case | Traceable inputs, formulas, scenarios, evidence, versioning, and reusable modeling assets |
| Account Executive | Advance the opportunity and align the buying group | A clear decision narrative, stakeholder relevance, next actions, and visible readiness blockers |
| Customer Champion | Validate operational reality and mobilize internal support | A simple way to correct facts, assumptions, owners, baselines, and evidence |
| Finance or Economic Buyer | Evaluate investment return and risk | Auditable cash flows, ROI, NPV, IRR, payback, sensitivity, assumptions, and confidence |
| Executive Approver | Make a decision quickly and responsibly | A concise outcome, investment, expected range, risks, evidence, and recommendation |
| Governance Reviewer | Prevent unsupported or unauthorized claims | Evidence state, provenance, freshness, approvals, exceptions, and complete audit history |
| Realization Owner | Measure whether promised value is achieved | Baselines, targets, actuals, variance, cadence, ownership, and immutable forecast history |
| Tenant Administrator or Operator | Keep the product secure and reliable | Tenant isolation, permissions, service health, trace IDs, audit events, and recoverability |

## Jobs-to-be-done

1. When I enter a new opportunity, help me turn incomplete account context into a transparent analysis plan without pretending that missing facts are known.
2. When the system detects pain and potential value, help me inspect the source, reasoning, confidence, and relevance before I accept it.
3. When I build the financial model, help me connect business outcomes to drivers, levers, variables, formulas, evidence, and scenario ranges.
4. When I collaborate with a customer, let us challenge assumptions and see downstream impact without losing the original source or approved history.
5. When I prepare an executive decision, generate a concise narrative from the exact reviewed model rather than from loose context.
6. When I publish or export, prove that the artifact is the exact authorized, approved, evidence-complete version.
7. After the decision, help me compare promised and realized value without rewriting the original commitment.

## Product success measures

| Measure | Definition | Target direction |
|---|---|---|
| Time to first defensible model | Elapsed time from case creation to a calculated model with traceable inputs and reviewed hypotheses | Decrease |
| Claim provenance coverage | Share of material financial and factual claims with complete source-to-claim lineage | Increase toward 100% |
| Human review coverage | Share of generated signals, hypotheses, and material assumptions with recorded disposition | Increase toward 100% |
| Approval cycle time | Elapsed time from calculated model to approved customer-ready version | Decrease without bypassing gates |
| Governed publication rate | Share of published cases with no open authorization, evidence, freshness, or review blocker | Maintain at 100% |
| Forecast variance | Difference between approved forecast and measured realized value over the agreed period | Understand and reduce |
| Reusable asset rate | Share of validated drivers, formulas, evidence, and benchmarks reused with visible applicability checks | Increase |

# Runbook Index

This root index exists for discoverability only. Keep incident procedures,
operational details, and service-specific response steps in the canonical
runbook locations.

## Canonical runbook references

- [Operations runbooks](docs/operations/runbooks/README.md) — current operational runbooks for service and platform response.
- [Documentation runbooks](docs/runbooks/README.md) — documentation-facing runbook catalog and supporting procedures.
- [Incident response](ops/incident/README.md) — incident severity, escalation, communications, and postmortem workflows.

During an incident, follow the applicable incident response procedure first,
then use the service-specific runbook linked from the canonical catalog.
